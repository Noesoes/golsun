Art, music and jingles in this folder come from the "Ninja Adventure" asset pack
by Pixel-Boy and AAA: https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack
Released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0). Thank you!

Only the pieces Rootwell uses are included; music was converted from OGG to MP3.
Rebuild with: python3 tools/build_assets.py "/path/to/Ninja Adventure - Asset Pack"

Battle monsters (assets/monsters) come from two free packs, used under their licenses
(free for personal and commercial use, no redistribution as standalone assets):
  - Pipoya Free RPG Monster Pack by Pipoya: https://pipoya.itch.io/free-rpg-monster-pack
    (slime, mushroom, ghost, wraith; downscaled and pixelated for this game)
  - Creature Collector 64x64 by dadddydarko: https://dadddydarko.itch.io/creature-collector-64x64-2d-various-creatures-with-different-themes
    (turtle, moth, squirrel, wasp, worm)
Rebuild with: python3 tools/build_monsters.py <Pipoya "non shade" folder> <"Static Creatures" folder>

Outdoor ground, paths, tall grass, bushes, big trees, rocks, signs and stone cliffs (assets/tiles/c_*.png)
come from "Pixel Art Top Down - Basic" by Cainos: https://cainos.itch.io/pixel-art-top-down-basic
(free for personal and commercial use, no redistribution as standalone assets).
water_c.png is the Ninja Adventure lake recoloured to match. Rebuild with tools/build_cainos.py.
